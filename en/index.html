SEO aIdeas — autonomous brand visibility auditor Own Project

A database-less analytical engine that replaces manually checking on an SEO/Ads agency's work. It pulls real Google rankings via the Serper API, compares them against competitors, and uses Claude to generate a prioritized action list — what to write, what to link, which keywords to close out.

  • Flags brand gaps — head terms where the brand should rank in the TOP 10 and doesn't.
  • Surfaces keywords at positions 11-20 that can be pushed to page one quickly.
  • Runs on flat CSV/JSON files — no database to maintain.
  • Terminal UI (TUI) with a live brand-visibility leaderboard.

A positioning audit that used to take 16 hours now takes 12 seconds, and it surfaced 248 keywords with untapped buying intent.
